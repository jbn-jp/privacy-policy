Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy describes how HubSpot Tag Slug Updater (the "App"), operated by JBN Co., Ltd. ("we", "us"), handles information when you connect your HubSpot account.

1. Information We Access

When you authorize the App via HubSpot OAuth, we access the following data from your HubSpot account solely to provide the App's functionality:

  • Blog tag names and slug values

We do not access contacts, deals, or any other HubSpot CRM data.

2. How We Use Your Information

The data accessed is used exclusively to display your blog tags and to update tag slug values at your explicit request. We do not use your data for any other purpose, including marketing or analytics.

3. Data Storage

We do not store your HubSpot data on our servers. Blog tag information is fetched in real time during your session and is not persisted after the session ends.

4. OAuth Tokens

OAuth access tokens and refresh tokens are stored as secure, HTTP-only cookies in your browser for the duration of your session. Tokens are never written to our databases or shared with third parties. You can revoke access at any time from your HubSpot account settings under Connected Apps.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your information to third parties.

6. Security

All communication between your browser, the App, and the HubSpot API is encrypted via HTTPS. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this URL. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
